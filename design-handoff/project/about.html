I started LED Technica in early 2024 because I was tired of bookmarking my own postmortems in a private wiki nobody else would ever see. The posts here are the ones I'd want to read — practical, opinionated, and short enough to finish on a transatlantic flight.

What this is

A blog about running real systems in the real cloud. Most posts fall into one of four buckets:

  • Postmortems and field notes from production incidents I've lived through.
  • Tutorials on the boring parts of the stack — Postgres, nginx, systemd, TLS.
  • Architecture notes on choices that worked and choices I'd undo.
  • Opinions, usually about complexity.

What this isn't

I don't write hot takes on the company-of-the-week. I don't write LLM coding listicles. I don't write thinkpieces about the future of work. There are better people than me writing those, and the world has enough of them.

How it's built

The site is statically generated with Astro, served from a single $6 VM behind Cloudflare. Type set in Geist and Geist Mono. UI components from Tailwind Catalyst, recolored with the three LEDs from the logo. The whole thing is open source — see the link in the sidebar.

Get in touch

Email is best. I read everything; I reply to most. If you'd like me to speak at your team meeting or write for your publication, mention "speaking" or "writing" in the subject line so I can find it later.
