01 — Foundations

Brand DNA

The blog is named for an LED. Three colored bulbs on a black field. The design system carries that idea forward: a quiet, neutral surface (zinc) with three accent LEDs (pink, yellow, cyan) used functionally — each maps to a category of post so the color tells you what kind of article you're looking at.

Quiet by default

Neutral zinc canvas. The page is mostly black and white. Color only appears when it carries meaning.

Functional accent

Each LED maps to a post category. The dots aren't decoration — they're the legend.

Technical voice

Monospaced metadata, generous line length, no breathy filler. The design respects the reader's time.

05 — Logo

2 states + hero treatment

The white-on-transparent mark sits inside a dark circular plate. On the home page it appears huge in the hero (un-lit by default; LEDs glow on hover) then animates into the navbar as the user scrolls. Everywhere else it's the small plated mark.

Default · powered on

saturated LEDs · subtle lift

Hover · yellow halo

single-color glow · disc + logo untouched

Navbar · 36px

no glow · 1px ring

Rules

  • • Always keep the dark plate. The white mark needs the black field to read like an LED display.
  • • Hero treatment: 168px diameter, ground halo behind, hover lights LEDs.
  • • Navbar treatment: 36-38px diameter, no halo, hairline ring.
  • • Don't apply the conic halo to the navbar mark — too much chrome.

10 — Prose & code

Reading column

An h2 inside the article body

Body paragraphs read at 17.2 pixels and 30.5 pixels of leading. Bold text picks up the foreground color. Inline links get an underline on hover that grows out from the left.

13 — Motion

Restrained, persistent

Hero logo scroll

Center → navbar

JS interpolates position + size based on how far the hero is scrolled out of viewport. 168px → 38px. cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1)

Logo LEDs

Powered on → bloom on hover

Default state is fully colored. On hover, a conic-gradient halo fades in (14s linear spin) and multi-color drop-shadows simulate LED light hitting the surface. Respects prefers-reduced-motion.

Headline underline

Grow from left

Background-image linear-gradient with animated background-size from 0 → 100% on hover. .35s.

FOUC-free theme script

<!-- ThemeScript.astro · in <head> -->
<script is:inline>
  const t = localStorage.getItem('ledtechnica:theme')
         || 'system';
  const dark = t === 'dark'
    || (t === 'system'
      && matchMedia('(prefers-color-scheme: dark)')
           .matches);
  document.documentElement.classList.toggle('dark', dark);
</script>
